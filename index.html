Memory & Encoding Study

How does the way we think about information affect memory?

Welcome

In this study, you will learn 18 words using three different strategies. After a short distraction activity, you will be asked to recall as many words as you can.

Before you begin
  • Find a quiet place and give the task your full attention.
  • Each word will appear on screen for a few seconds.
  • Do not write down or photograph the words.
  • At the end, type every word you remember. The order does not matter.

A participant code is optional. Do not enter your name or other identifying information.
